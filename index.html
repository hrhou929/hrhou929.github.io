A little about me

Hello, I'm Haoran.

I'm interested in how AI agents work in practice, and how we can tell when they work well.

I am an undergraduate studying Statistics and Data Science at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, graduating in June 2027. My interests include agent systems, LLM evaluation, and multimodal AI.

At CUHK's ARISE Lab, I contributed to VisualRepair, a framework for repairing software issues using visual evidence. More recently, I worked on agent evaluation and reusable workflows as an AI Product Engineer Intern at Meituan's Meyo Agent Platform.

My statistics background keeps me asking what an evaluation actually measures, how stable its results are, and what we can learn from failures. I enjoy turning those questions into experiments and working systems.

AI Product Engineer Intern · Meyo Agent Platform

Research experience

VisualRepair

Sep 2025 – Apr 2026
Research Assistant · CUHK Faculty of Engineering, ARISE Lab

Contributed to a multimodal repair framework that combines repositories, issue descriptions, and visual attachments to understand software bugs and evaluate candidate patches.

  • Worked on Image Type-aware Tool Calling, including GIF key-frame extraction, OCR, irrelevant-region cropping, and code-template retrieval.
  • Contributed to Dynamic Test-time Region Focusing and the design and debugging of the repair workflow.
  • Worked on automated patch selection using compilation checks, rendered screenshots, visual comparison, and MLLM-based evaluation.
  • Conducted experiments, ablations, and error analysis. The final method resolved 196 of 517 SWE-bench Multimodal test instances (37.91%).
