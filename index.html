Hi, I’m Haoran Hou.

I test how reliably AI agents complete tasks and analyze where they fail.

I study Statistics and Data Science at The Chinese University of Hong Kong and expect to graduate in June 2027. I’m interested in what an evaluation actually measures, how stable the results are, and what an agent’s failures can teach us.

At CUHK’s ARISE Lab, I contributed to VisualRepair, a framework that uses visual evidence to repair software issues. At Meituan’s Meyo Agent Platform, I worked on agent evaluation and reusable workflows.

Experience & Education

Research

  1. VisualRepair

    Sep 2025 – Apr 2026
    Research Assistant · CUHK Faculty of Engineering, ARISE Lab

    Contributed to a multimodal repair framework that combines repositories, issue descriptions, and visual attachments to evaluate candidate patches. The final method resolved 196 of 517 SWE-bench Multimodal test instances (37.91%).

    Research contributions
    • Worked on Image Type-aware Tool Calling, including GIF key-frame extraction, OCR, irrelevant-region cropping, and code-template retrieval.
    • Contributed to Dynamic Test-time Region Focusing and the design and debugging of the repair workflow.
    • Worked on automated patch selection using compilation checks, rendered screenshots, visual comparison, and MLLM-based evaluation.
    • Conducted experiments, ablations, and error analysis.
